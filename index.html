Show Me & Co. Policy

1.Scope

This website operates as part of the Show Me & Co. studio practice. It presents our work, our process, and an admission form for prospective clients and collaborators.

By submitting the admission form, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

2.Data Collection

When you submit the admission form, we collect only the information you choose to share:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your country and city
  • Any context you provide about yourself and your project

We do not collect data passively, and we do not use tracking cookies.

3.Operational Interfaces

Forms and submission interfaces presented on this website are operational. Submissions are delivered securely through Web3Forms, an email delivery service, directly to our studio inbox.

Where additional services are introduced in the future, they will be clearly identified and separate terms may apply.

4.How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to review your admission request, respond to you personally, and understand the context of your project.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

5.Data Storage

Your submission is delivered to us securely and retained only as long as necessary to review and respond to your request. You may request deletion at any time.

6.Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time. Write to us and we will respond promptly.

7.Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us at the address below.
